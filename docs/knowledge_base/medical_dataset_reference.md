## Who wrote the v2 reasoning (from dataset cards and papers)

| Source | Generator | Verification the publisher states |
|---|---|---|
| UltraMedical | GPT-4 | none for the trace; the v2 build kept only traces that agree with the answer field |
| medical-o1 | GPT-4o | a "medical verifier" |
| Medical-R1-Distill | DeepSeek-R1 | not stated |
| MedReason | an LLM the card and paper do not name, from knowledge-graph paths; written knowing the answer | paths "validated for consistency with clinical logic" |
| ReasonMed | three unnamed LLMs | multi-agent check; the v2 build read the training letter from the trace itself |
| MedMCQA, MedQA, PubMedQA | exam explanations, answer keys, heuristic labels | exam keys; PubMedQA artificial labels are heuristic |

**None of the v2 reasoning was written by Qwen3.** E2's reasoning is
written only by Qwen3-4B itself.

## Audit findings that matter for training ([measured], 2026-10-03)

- **Style.** 67% of reasoning rows (MedReason, UltraMedical, ReasonMed) are
  written unlike Qwen3: 0–1% open in the first person (Qwen3: 100%), and
  0.01–0.08 self-checks per 1,000 words (Qwen3: 7.1).
- **Nested think tokens.** 135 ReasonMed rows nest a second
  `<think>…</think>` block, which trains real special tokens mid-thought.
- **Contradictions.** Some MedReason traces conclude against their training
  letter (two confirmed by hand, e.g. medreason-10693).
- **Circular agreement.** The UltraMedical and ReasonMed agreement figures
  verify nothing.
- **Length.** Training traces have medians of 285–800 tokens (ReasonMed
  1,324), against base Qwen3's ≥ 898 at a 1,536 cap.
- **Open.** Whether the medical reasoning is correct has not been reviewed by
  a person or a judge model. Gold-letter filtering, E2's included, cannot
  catch a right answer reached by wrong reasoning. A rehearsal on Qwen3-0.6B
  produced exactly that case.
